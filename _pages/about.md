I'm an industrial PhD student researching machine learning algorithms for money laundering, with a specific focus on graph-based methods and trade-based money laundering. 
            At the University of Copenhagen (Department of Computer Science), I'm supervised by [Oswin Krause](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=5ER3NYoAAAAJ&hl=en) and 
            [Raghavendra Selvan](https://raghavian.github.io/). At the Danish Financial Intelligence Unit, I'm supervised by Dorte Hermansen.
            In the spring of 2026 I was a visiting researcher at Universiteit Utrecht (School of Economics), where I was hosted by [Joras Ferwerda](https://www.uu.nl/staff/    JFerwerda).
My PhD is partly funded by a "Erhversforsker" grant from Innovation Fund Denmark and partly from the Danish FIU.
